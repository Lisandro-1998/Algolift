FAQ

What builders ask

If your question is not here, write to us — we reply within 24 hours.

How much time does working together cost me?

About three hours up front for the assessment and signing off the strategy. After that, roughly 20 minutes a month to approve the finished content.

Do we have to film ourselves?

Yes. Your team captures the live jobsite on their phones — after a short briefing from us. We turn the raw footage into finished content. That way you never depend on scheduled shoots.

Which regions do you work in?

We serve trade, construction and timber companies across South Tyrol, Switzerland, Austria and southern Germany, some across several locations. The collaboration runs remotely.

Which channels do you run?

LinkedIn for clients, architects and investors, Instagram for regional visibility. Both channels are handled separately, not cross-posted.

How long until the first result?

The first published project takes around six weeks. After that we publish monthly, with a review once a quarter.

What happens in the first call?

30 minutes, free. You get an honest assessment of your presence and see examples from our work. No sales pitch. If it does not add up for you, we say so in the same call.
